Instructions

Dotwright is for practising writing braille, with feedback on contractions as you go. Choose how to write with the switch under the page.

Brailler mode

Write the way you would on a braillewriter, with six keys on your keyboard. Press the keys of a cell together, then let go. The braille appears as you'd read it, with the print over each word.

Space and Enter work as on a brailler, and finishing a word with either shows its feedback. Backspace, the arrow keys and undo work as usual. You can change the six keys in Settings.

Slate mode

Write the way you would with a slate and stylus. The page is turned over, as on a real slate: it's mirrored, lines run from right to left, and dots are pressed in. There's no feedback until you switch back to Brailler mode, just as you'd take the paper out and turn it over to check it.

Tap or click dots on the slate under the page to set them, then Next to write the cell and move on. Next with no dots writes a space. Delete takes back the last cell, and New line starts the next line. Tap the page to move the cursor. The keyboard only moves the cursor in Slate mode, so you practise with the slate.

On a phone, use Slate mode, unless you have a keyboard connected.

Lines and pages

Lines are 40 cells and pages are 25 lines, as in a BRF file. As on a brailler, a bell rings near the end of a line, and a full line takes nothing more until you start a new one. If a word doesn't fit, delete it and write it on the next line.

Feedback

In Brailler mode, a word with a contraction error is underlined with a wavy line, and the print over it shows how it reads. Click or tap the word to see the correct form. With a screen reader, moving onto the word says "contraction error".

Tab from the braille goes to the print view: the print of each line, to read through. Moving in it moves the braille cursor to the same word.

Saving your work

Save a BRF file to keep your work. Dotwright also keeps a copy in this browser in case the page is closed or refreshed, but only in this browser on this device, and it's lost if browser data is cleared. Your braille never leaves your device.

On a phone, New, Open, Save, Settings and these instructions are in the menu at the top.
