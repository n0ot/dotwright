Instructions

Writing

Press the keys of a cell together, then release them.

  • Enter starts a new line. Space or Enter finishes a word, and feedback for it appears then.
  • Lines are 40 cells. A word that would pass cell 40 moves to a new line; pages are 25 lines.
  • Arrow keys, Backspace, Delete, Shift selection, cut, copy and paste work as usual.
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; add Shift to redo.

Slate

The slate at the bottom of the screen writes one cell at a time, at the cursor. Tap or click a dot to punch it, and again to take it out. Next moves on to the next cell; on an empty cell, it writes a space. Previous cell goes back a cell so you can change it. New line starts the next line. Tap or click the page to move the cursor.

The page has two sides, and the slate is always on the same side as the page. The front is the reading side: cells are as you read them, and print and feedback are shown. The back is the side you write on with a real slate: the page and the slate are mirrored, so dots 1, 2 and 3 are on the right, and the line runs from right to left. Dots on the back are pits, and there's no feedback until you turn the page over.

  • The Front and Back buttons under the slate turn the page over. Alt+F also turns it over.
  • The keyboard keeps working alongside the slate. Settings can hide the slate.

Braille keys

Alt+B changes which keys enter dots 1 to 6, to suit your keyboard layout. The space bar always enters a space.

Feedback

  • Words with a contraction error are underlined with a wavy line. Moving onto one announces "contraction error", and moving off it announces "out of contraction error".
  • Alt+W: print and contraction status of the word at the cursor.
  • Alt+R: show the correct form of the word at the cursor. Escape hides it. With a mouse, click the word and use Show correct form.
  • Alt+N: next contraction error; Alt+Shift+N: previous.
  • Tab from the document goes to the print view: a read-only copy of the print interlining, line for line. Moving through it moves the braille cursor to the same word, and the commands above work there too.
  • Alt+T: turn feedback (print and contraction indicators) on or off.

Document

  • Alt+L: page, line and cell of the cursor.
  • Alt+G: go to a line and cell. Lines are counted from the start of the document, so line 26 is the first line of page 2.
  • Alt+K: turn announcing each entered cell on or off. This and showing empty dot positions are also in Settings.
  • Ctrl/Cmd+O: open a BRF file. Ctrl/Cmd+S: save a BRF file.
  • Alt+H: these instructions.

Saving and recovery

Save a BRF file to keep your work. This browser also keeps a recovery copy in case the page is refreshed, but it stays only in this browser on this device and is lost if browser data is cleared. Your braille never leaves your device.
