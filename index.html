Instructions

Practise writing contracted braille, with feedback on each word. Choose how to write with the switch under the page.

On a phone without a keyboard, write in Slate mode, then switch to Brailler mode to check it.

Brailler mode

Write as on a braillewriter: press the keys of a cell together, then let go. The keys can be changed in Settings.

A word is checked when you finish it with Space or Enter. Backspace, the arrow keys and undo work as usual.

In Settings, feedback can instead come as each cell is written, showing how the word reads so far, or once each line is finished.

Slate mode

Write as with a slate and stylus: the page is mirrored and lines run right to left. There's no feedback until you switch back to Brailler mode, as you'd turn the paper over to check it.

Tap or click dots to set them, then Next to write the cell. Next with no dots writes a space, Delete erases the last cell, and New line starts the next line. Dot 1 is at the top right; dot numbers can be shown in Settings.

Tap the page to move the cursor. The keyboard only moves it, and the arrow keys go the way they point on the mirrored page.

Lines and pages

Lines are 40 cells and pages 25 lines. A bell rings near the end of a line, and a full line takes nothing more: if a word doesn't fit, delete it and write it on the next line.

Feedback

The print over each word shows how it reads. A word with a contraction error has a wavy underline: click or tap it to see the correct form. With a screen reader, moving onto it says "contraction error".

Tab goes to the print view, to read the print line by line. The braille cursor follows it.

Saving your work

Save a BRF file to keep your work. If the page is closed or refreshed, it's restored from a copy in this browser, which is lost if browser data is cleared. Nothing is sent anywhere.
